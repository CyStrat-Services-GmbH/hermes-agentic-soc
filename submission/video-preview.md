# Private product video: first draft

A 76-second German product-video draft accompanies review release 0.1.1 as a separate delivery artifact. It is not embedded in the plugin distribution, uploaded to X or published publicly. Repository visibility stays private.

## Content

- Evidence-first positioning and six defense specialist roles.
- 39 skills (including a nine-skill THOR analysis suite) and 25 technology scaffolds, plus CyStrat SOC Suite as the cross-domain platform.
- Real offline CLI results from synthetic THOR evidence: four events, three observation groups, provisional P2/suspicious.
- Strict separation between observation, suspicion and unsupported conclusions.
- THOR as a separately installed external tool; no bundled scanner/signatures.
- exploit labs and managing director Johannes Schönborn as the planned contributor of a **future** offensive-research release; nothing of it is shown because nothing of it ships.
- Operator-disabled preview, scope checks and draft-only response actions.

## Production

1920×1080, 16:9, 24 fps, H.264/yuv420p, German synthetic narration and a quiet original synthesized sound bed. Editorial typography and motion graphics use actual demo values; the terminal panel is explicitly a condensed presentation of actual results, not a captured Hermes UI or a live THOR scan.

No third-party logos were recreated. Partner names are text credits only. No malware, customer telemetry, real incident evidence or operative offensive capability is shown.

The narration text is `video-voiceover-de.txt`. The rendered MP4, SRT and editable renderer are retained separately in the private project deliverables. Before a public submission, review partner wording, final license status, caption sync, demo requirements and publication approval.
