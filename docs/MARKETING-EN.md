# Hermes Agentic SOC

When agentic AI goes on the offensive, the world needs a new kind of defense. Hermes Agentic SOC brings cybersecurity expertise into the agent workflow, with human responsibility at its center.

## Core message

Cybersecurity from Germany. For the world. Your tools, your data and your security stack, supported by specialized skills, native tools, workflow references and expert profiles built from our cybersecurity experience.

## The product story

- **The challenge:** Frame the opening as a product vision for a world of offensive agentic AI. Do not claim that the MVP autonomously stops AI-driven attacks.
- **The expertise:** CyStrat Services and Nextron Systems unite defense and forensics in specialized Hermes workflows. Profiles guide behavior; they are not independent certified experts or security sandboxes.
- **Available today:** Analyze supported local THOR exports and supplied alerts, connect observations conservatively and produce traceable first assessments.
- **The roadmap:** 21 technology templates span SIEM, SOAR/case management, EDR/XDR/DFIR, threat intelligence, detection engineering and CyStrat SOC Suite. Templates exist today; verified live remote connectors do not.
- **The preview:** Offensive Security is the planned contribution of exploit labs, led by managing director Johannes Schönborn. Only the disabled research skeleton, preview metadata and marketing credit are included.
- **The promise of control:** Read-only planned integrations, explicit scope and human approval for sensitive actions. No autonomous containment or production-write connector is shipped.

## Trailer direction

The English trailer opens with a cinematic illustrative network globe and a short threat/defense narrative. It then shows the current MVP, the named integration roadmap, contributor roles and human control before returning to the international tagline.

Voice-over, screen typography and subtitles are English. Report excerpts and sample counts are derived from the executed synthetic fixture, not a real customer incident; no vendor UI or logo is fabricated.

## Publication gate

This is an internal review asset, not a public launch. Repository visibility, the final license, external publication and catalog/hackathon submission require renewed explicit authorization.
